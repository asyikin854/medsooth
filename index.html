About Medsooth™

Health footwear made for long shifts on your feet

Medsooth™ health shoes are built with a Poron® anti-bacterial insole, Coolmax® mesh lining, an orthotic arch system and a natural rubber outsole that is slip-resistant and light — designed for nurses, uniformed personnel, teachers and anyone who stands or walks for long hours.

Alongside Medsooth™, our parent company Stealthmed Ventures supplies nurse tudung and caps, Ministry of Health apparel, textiles, hospital linen and complete uniforms and accessories for uniformed services.
